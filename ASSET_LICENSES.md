# Asset and dependency provenance

## Project material

The project code, generic example writing, procedural sample illustrations, document cards, and neutral replacement objects in this public edition are distributed under the root MIT license. The project was developed with AI assistance. This notice does not claim third-party review or a history of external contributions.

The public edition derives its desk layout and app interactions from the author's private room prototype. Private photos and personal document content are not part of this edition. Models containing embedded source images were rebuilt without those images, and game-derived geometry/animations and recognizable character objects were replaced by neutral procedural objects.

`dist/assets/PUBLIC_ASSET_SOURCES.json` provides a machine-readable record for sample data and font files. Model processing details are in `docs/VERIFICATION.md`. Some filenames and node names remain as compatibility identifiers; they are not claims of brand affiliation or permission to reuse the earlier private materials.

## Three.js

The bundled Three.js modules and add-ons are version 0.180.0, under the MIT license. The original notice is retained at `dist/vendor/three.LICENSE`. Imports were adjusted for local relative paths.

## Fonts

The bundled `.woff2` files are covered by their accompanying SIL Open Font License notices:

- `dist/assets/fonts/OFL.txt`
- `dist/assets/fonts/OFL-NotoSerif.txt`

Those files and their notices are retained from the source project without modification. Font licensing remains separate from the project's MIT license.

## Adding material

For any new asset, record its source, author, license and modifications before inclusion. Do not infer permission from public availability. User-supplied personal content belongs in a private customization unless its publication and redistribution are explicitly intended.
